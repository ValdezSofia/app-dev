# Coraline (2009)

> *"Be careful what you wish for."*

---

### Overview
While exploring her new home, an adventurous girl named Coraline finds a secret door that leads to an alternate, idealized version of her life. However, she soon discovers that sinister secrets lurk behind the button-eyed facade of this parallel world.

### Key Details
- **Director:** Henry Selick
- **Based on:** *Coraline* by Neil Gaiman
- **Studio:** Laika Entertainment
- **Genre:** Dark Fantasy / Stop-Motion Animation / Horror
- **Run Time:** 100 minutes

### Main Characters & Voice Cast
1. Dakota Fanning as *Coraline Jones*
2. Teri Hatcher as *Mel Jones / The Other Mother (Beldam)*
3. John Hodgman as *Charlie Jones / The Other Father*
4. Keith David as *The Cat*
5. Robert Bailey Jr. as *Wybie Lovat*

### Why It's a Favorite
* **Stop-Motion Craftsmanship:** Incredible handmade sets and miniature puppet animation that hold up over time.
* **Atmosphere & Music:** Bruno Coulais' eerie score and the uncanny blend of whimsical and horrifying visuals.
* **Themes:** Highlights courage, appreciating what you have, and the danger of superficial perfection.
